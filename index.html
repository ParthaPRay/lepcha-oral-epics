The sanctioned project

Documenting Lepcha Oral Epics and Environmental Narratives of Sikkim through Digital Recording and Knowledge Mapping

A community-centred digital documentation project that records selected oral narratives and organises them with context, place and attribution.

Project objectives

What the project sets out to do

  1. 01

    To create a culturally respectful and reliable record of selected Lepcha oral epics and environmental narratives through audio and video documentation, preserving both spoken content and performative elements such as tone, pauses, gestures and setting.

  2. 02

    To organise the recorded material into a structured digital archive with basic descriptive information including location, narrative type, thematic focus and contextual notes provided by narrators.

  3. 03

    To develop a simple web-based map linking narratives to specific ecological and cultural locations, highlighting the place-based nature of Lepcha knowledge.
